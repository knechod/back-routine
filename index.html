Back Maintenance Routine

⏱ 15 min · 3×/week 🧘 Warm-up daily 🎯 Prevent flare-ups
B.01.02 — 4-Week Progressive Plan
Rule Pain-free only  ·  Light weights  ·  Slow control  ·  Stop if sharp pain occurs
Every Day · 0–3 Minutes
Warm-Up learn more ↗
Do this every morning, even on rest days
  • Standing pelvic tilts – 10 slow reps
  • Shoulder rolls (backward) – 5 reps
  • Gentle torso turns – 5 reps each side

Mon · Wed · Fri  |  Bodyweight only · Learn the movements · ~8 min total

3–5 min
